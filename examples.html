Selected Projects

Examples of focused engineering work delivered in complex operating environments.

Production Optimization

Decline Rate Stabilization Program

Challenge

Rapid production decline across mature wells with limited intervention windows.

Approach

Integrated nodal analysis, lift strategy tuning, and prioritized well candidate ranking.

Result

Improved production stability and reduced avoidable intervention cycles.

Flow Assurance

Offshore Throughput Reliability Upgrade

Challenge

Recurring pressure drops and slugging behavior during high-demand periods.

Approach

Steady-state and transient diagnostics, operating envelope review, and control-setpoint updates.

Result

Smoother operating profile with fewer flow interruptions and better planning confidence.

Integrity & Risk

Pipeline Risk Prioritization Study

Challenge

Multiple integrity threats with constrained maintenance budget and inspection capacity.

Approach

Risk-based segmentation, criticality scoring, and phased inspection recommendations.

Result

Clear integrity roadmap aligned to budget and operational risk tolerance.

Process Debottlenecking

Compression Constraint Relief

Challenge

Compressor train bottlenecks limiting gas handling and forcing conservative operation.

Approach

Constraint mapping, quick-win modifications, and staged engineering for long-term uplift.

Result

Increased system flexibility and improved utilization of available processing capacity.
